## sunday, 17.05.2026

**NOTES ON _MIXTAPE_**

not even 10 minutes in, _Just Like Honey_ starts playing. I'm sold already,
although idk if it's the best mood-setter.

walking around feels a bit like a museum tour for nostalgia-generating tokens.

tiny interactable details like QTE for handshake and rewinding a casete tape
with the joystick make the experience feel more grounded and analogic.

IS THIS SOME KISSING GAMEPLAY?!?!?

love the detail of every character headbanging their own way, at whatever
tempo you want.

the game is doing a good job representing how stuff *feels* - images,
flashbacks, etc all give a lot to the emotional context. it kinda feels like
"default" emotions, though? like, not really anyone's, but some shared
imaginaria instead?

"loudness is a precious commodity; you gotta earn loudness."

this game is gorgeous; it feels like playing a selection of music videos. art
direction is really on point.

"people who have passions are hot"

the game kinda touches a lot of conflicts and worries teens have, but does so
in a pretty shallow way. Like, being sort of mysterious about it, just like a
teenager would do, but refusing to elaborate further.

KAIJU MODE

the "optimal teenage experience" does not exist. we all get it wrong... and
right at the same time.

I mean, yeah. Rockford's been sort of a prick. Cass has a solid point there.

_More than this_, by Roxy Music. another one straight to the chest.

Mate, what a show.


## saturday, 07.06.2025

I've recently dusted off my PlayStation 3, and played some _PES 2011_.

Man, what a game.

Even though controls are kinda rough, and fluidity can sometimes be
(almost) non-existent... this game feels so _pure_.

Quick game mode, tournament mode, and manager/player career mode, check
every imaginable boxes if you want to play a soccer game; UI is sharp
and straight to the point; and any distraction from the actual _play_ is
kept to the bare minimum.

I wish mainstream games could _un_-evolve back to that simplicity.
